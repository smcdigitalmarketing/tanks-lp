# Competitor analysis: Maggie's Tanks

Source: [maggies-tanks.com](https://maggies-tanks.com/), main product [Built-in Bra Comfort Tank](https://maggies-tanks.com/products/maggies-built-in-comfort-bra-tanktop). Researched 9–10 Nov 2026 from the public pages, the public product JSON, the page HTML and the public Judge.me review widget. Mobile was checked in a 390 px browser. Nothing here was bought, and checkout was not attempted.

Everything below describes the competitor. Anything stated as fact about the product is **their claim**; see [product-research.md](product-research.md) for the claims register and what's verified.

## The short version

- **Product:** a women's nylon/spandex tank with a built-in bra: soft cups at the front, wide straps, no underwire, no hooks, no band. 13 colours, S–4XL, $34.95.
- **Offer:** quantity bundles carry the economics. 1 for $34.95, "Buy 2 Get 1 Free" (3 for $69.90), "Buy 3 Get 2 Free" (5 for $104.85), each tank chosen separately by colour and size.
- **Audience:** women roughly 55+ (every model and video reviewer is in that range: "Kim B., 71", "Donna C., 78") who find bras painful, with a strong secondary pull for fuller busts and larger sizes.
- **Persuasion:** pain-agitation copy ("Here's why bras stopped working for you"), before/after photos, a founder story, video testimonials, a comparison table scored 8/8, a fit guarantee, and heavy urgency: a countdown, "Low Stock - Selling Fast", and stock words inside colour names.
- **The gap:** the page says **"Excellent 4.8/5 (100,000+ happy customers)"**. The store's own Judge.me widget reports **3.20 out of 5 from 10,795 reviews**: 36% 5★, 27% 1★. The newest 200 or so reviews are 96% 5★, which makes the older reviews far more negative than the recent ones. Recurring complaints are fit (cups too small or too low, riding up), raw unhemmed edges, and size exchanges that weren't free.

### The buy box

- Gallery of 19 product images (mostly 1024 px, several apparently AI-generated). The caption on the first image reads "Maggie is 5'6" and 137 lbs, and wears M", and a "#1 BESTSELLER OF 2026" banner sits below the gallery, with no source given.
- Four bullets: no back bulge or bra lines; "padding that actually stays, tested through every wash"; "works like shapewear, tucks into high-waisted bottoms"; buttery-soft fabric. On mobile they add "Double lined, so nothing shows through" and "Breathable fabric that stays cool, even in summer".
- A size finder (height, weight, bust over the bra) that recommends a size.
- "Low Stock - Selling Fast", then three bundle cards. Each tank in a bundle gets its own **colour dropdown and size dropdown**, and the colour names include stock warnings ("Coffee (running low)").
- Inside the bundle: a free leakproof-underwear add-on ($24.95 struck through, "FREE") and a collagen-gummy subscription offered as a "free gift, first month".
- Under the button: "Ships by Tomorrow", geo-targeted "Free Shipping to KE", and a 90-Day Perfect Fit Guarantee badge.
- Mobile: a sticky bar with "Buy 2, Get 1 Free · $69.90 ~~$104.85~~ · ADD TO CART" is visible from the first screen.
- Theme code: the bundle builder is custom (`#bundle-section-custom`, `window.bbProductData`), not Kaching. Other apps: Judge.me (reviews, star badge hidden), Klaviyo, ParcelPanel tracking, Zobi popups, AppLovin.

## Customer journey

1. **Ad or social** (Facebook video testimonials, TikTok @maggiestanksofficial) → homepage or product page. Both pages carry the full sales letter.
2. **First screen:** countdown, rating claim, a result-led headline ("Have you lost weight?"), "Save 70%".
3. **Scroll:** before/afters → dream outcome → "it's not your body, it's the design" → why alternatives fail → buy box.
4. **Buy box:** pick a bundle (the 3-pack is pre-selected as "Most Popular"), then a colour and size per tank through dropdowns, accept the free underwear, add to cart.
5. **Reassurance after the button:** founder quote, size chart, video reviews, mechanism, routine, comparison, guarantee, FAQ, then a final bundle button.
6. **Cart drawer** with a sticky bar. A rule added on 5 Nov removes $0 gift lines once no paid item is left. Then Shopify checkout (not attempted).
7. **After purchase:** sizing exchanges handled by email. One free replacement size; returns need prior approval.

## What we deliberately did not copy

- Countdown timers, "Low Stock - Selling Fast", stock words in colour names, the "#1 Bestseller" badge.
- The 4.8/5 and 100,000+ customer claims, and the $84.95 compare-at price behind "Save 70%".
- Weight-loss, lift and "look ten years younger" claims, and before/after body photos.
- The unverified founder story and the Facebook video testimonials.
- Upsells of unrelated products (collagen gummies) and the free underwear gift, which would need its own product and stock.

## Reviews in detail

- Judge.me, product id 8733905256619: **10,795 reviews, average 3.20**. Breakdown: 5★ 36%, 4★ 13%, 3★ 14%, 2★ 11%, 1★ 27%.
- In the newest 210 reviews, 202 are 5★: a sharp change from the historical distribution. One "5★" review on this product is about a lipstick, so some imported reviews are mismatched.
- Common positive themes: you forget you're wearing it, comfort all day, enough support for everyday (several say "not for running"), service sorted out size exchanges.
- Common negative themes: cups too small or sitting too low, rides up on long torsos, raw unhemmed edges, size exchange not free in practice, fabric too warm.
- We use 12 reviews, word for word, as **labelled placeholders** (selection rule in [asset-manifest.md](asset-manifest.md)). Production needs our own reviews.
